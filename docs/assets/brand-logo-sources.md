# Storefront brand logo sources

The storefront brand marquee uses local, optimized copies so it does not depend on third-party image hosts at runtime. All marks remain the property of their respective trademark owners and are used only to identify catalogue filters. Supplier/manufacturer approval remains a pre-launch requirement.

## Recognized marks

- ABRO: official ABRO site, `https://abro.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ABRO-logo.svg`
- Caltex: Wikimedia Commons, `Caltex_brand_logo.svg`
- Castrol: Wikimedia Commons, `Castrol_textlogo.svg`
- Delphi, Honda, Mitsubishi, Suzuki and Toyota: Simple Icons project SVG exports
- Eurol, Petromin, Servo and Totachi: SeekLogo PNG exports, normalized to WebP
- Fuji: official Fuji Filters website logo
- LAUGFS: official LAUGFS Holdings website logo
- MAG: MAG Lubricants mark published by EasyUAE, normalized to WebP
- Mobil, Valvoline and Würth: Wikimedia Commons SVG exports
- Prestone: FastExpo vendor logo, normalized to WebP
- Royal Super: official Royal Super Lubricants website logo
- Sakura: Sakura Filters product mark, normalized to WebP
- Sinopec: public Sinopec corporate mark, normalized to WebP
- VIC: PartsPro VIC collection mark, normalized to WebP
- Waxpol: official Waxpol website logo

## Exact-name catalogue lockups

`Petrons`, `Prime`, `Pure Fast` and `Smash` are exact report-category names whose public identity could not be matched confidently to an authoritative downloadable mark. Their local SVGs are intentionally restrained name lockups, not claims of an official manufacturer logo. Replace them after supplier confirmation.

The footer oil-flow footage was supplied by the project owner from a Vecteezy download and optimized locally for web delivery. Confirm the owner’s Vecteezy license and any required attribution before public launch.
